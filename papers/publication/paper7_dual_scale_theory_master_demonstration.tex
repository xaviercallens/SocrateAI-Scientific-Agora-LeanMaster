\begin{abstract}
We present a Lean~4 companion formalization for a dual-scale scenario of type~II string theory on
$K3\times T^2$, and we state precisely what is, and is not, machine-checked. The Mathlib-free core of
the Lean artifact (51 files, 554 declarations, toolchain \texttt{v4.33.1}, \textbf{no external
dependency}, standard axioms only --- \texttt{\#print axioms} logs in \Cref{app:axioms}) certifies
exact integer and rational identities used in the argument: the positivity of the dual scale
$R+\alpha'/R$ on integer radii; the $K3$ topological invariants $\chi=24$, $\sigma=-16$,
$\mathrm{ind}(\slashed D)=2$; the elliptic-genus arithmetic $\mathcal A_2\cdot 60 = 4\mathcal
A_1\cdot 77 = 27720$ with $|M_{24}| = 27720\cdot 8832$; and a corrected, genuinely unique-minimum toy
potential (an earlier revision of this corpus contained a \texttt{Nat}-truncation bug that made the
claimed minimum non-unique; \Cref{sec:compactification} and \Cref{app:ledger} document the fix).
Two further Lean libraries, built on Mathlib (which contributes no axioms beyond Lean's own three
standard ones for the fragments used here), extend this core: \texttt{StringTheoryFormalization}
(Stream~1's sixth library, corrects the $M_{24}$ representation table used in the moonshine
arithmetic, \Cref{sec:moonshine}) and the new \texttt{DualScaleStream2} library (99 theorems, 19
modules), which formalizes the $O(d,d;\mathbb Z)$ T-duality group, the Double Field Theory
generalized metric, and the $K3\times T^2$ lattice layer (\Cref{sec:stream2}); together with the
original core, the audited total is 326 theorems (Stream~1, six libraries) and 99 theorems
(Stream~2), 0 failing, standard axioms only. The differential-geometric, index-theoretic and
cosmological statements built on top of this arithmetic --- Double Field Theory, the
Atiyah--Singer index theorem, moduli stabilization, and the tensor-to-scalar ratio, CP phase, and
dark-energy predictions --- are either drawn from the literature or proposed here as conjectures,
and are labeled by epistemic tier throughout (\tierA\ / \tierL\ / \tierC). In particular we discuss
the obstruction that $\mathcal{N}=4$ non-renormalization poses for moduli stabilization on $K3\times
T^2$, and we confront the paper's speculative numerical relations for $r$, $\delta_{\mathrm{CP}}$,
and $(w_0,w_a)$ with current data, including the DESI DR2 preference for a time-evolving dark-energy
equation of state at $3.1\sigma$ over $(w_0,w_a)=(-1,0)$. We close with a roadmap for promoting the
central real-analytic claims (currently Tier L or Tier C) to kernel-checked Tier A theorems using
Mathlib --- a roadmap Stream~2 has already begun executing.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction: The Landscape Problem and the Scope of This Paper}
\label{sec:intro}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

The pursuit of a consistent, unified quantum theory of gravity has constituted a central goal of
theoretical high-energy physics for over half a century. Superstring theory provides an anomaly-free
perturbative framework uniting non-abelian gauge fields with general relativity in ten spacetime
dimensions, but its physical realization in four dimensions confronts the \textbf{Landscape
Problem}: in traditional flux compactifications (e.g.\ the KKLT and Large Volume Scenarios), an
estimated $10^{500}$ metastable vacua arise from the interplay of hundreds of geometric moduli with
under-constrained flux choices \cite{Vafa2005, Douglas2003, KKLT2003}. In the absence of a
stabilizing mechanism, these moduli remain massless, generating unphysical fifth forces and leaving
gauge couplings, Yukawa hierarchies, and the cosmological constant undetermined.

Separately, modern theoretical physics faces a practical \textbf{verification problem}: papers in
string theory and cosmology routinely involve long chains of tensor algebra, differential forms, and
representation-theoretic bookkeeping that are tedious and error-prone to check by hand. Interactive
theorem provers such as Lean~4 \cite{Lean4Paper} offer a way to make specific algebraic claims within
such an argument kernel-checkable.

This paper is a case study in doing exactly that --- for a restricted, honestly-scoped set of claims
about a proposed \textbf{Dual-Scale} scenario for type~II string theory compactified on $K3\times
T^2$. We adopt the following convention throughout, adapted from the tiered-evidence discipline used
elsewhere in this research programme:

\subsection{Epistemic tiers}
\label{sec:tiers}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \tierA\ --- \textbf{Lean kernel}: the Lean~4 kernel accepted the exact statement, with no
    \texttt{sorry}, and its axiom footprint is only the three standard Lean axioms
    (\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; verified in
    \Cref{app:axioms}). Tier A is a claim about a specific formal statement, not about the informal
    sentence next to it in this paper --- \Cref{app:ledger} gives the exact Lean type for every
    Tier A claim so the reader can check the two match.
  \item \tierL\ --- \textbf{literature}: an established result quoted from a peer-reviewed source,
    not independently re-derived or re-verified here.
  \item \tierC\ --- \textbf{conjecture / proposal}: a claim original to this paper, or a numerical
    pattern-match, that is neither kernel-checked nor an established literature result. Tier~C
    claims are falsifiable proposals, not derivations.
\end{itemize}
A claim may not rest, even transitively, on a claim of a lower tier without that dependency being
stated. Adjectives (``rigorous'', ``striking'', ``decisive'') are never a substitute for a tier.

\section{Double Field Theory and \texorpdfstring{$O(D,D)$}{O(D,D)} Generalized Riemannian Geometry}
\label{sec:dft}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

Standard Riemannian geometry treats spacetime as a pseudo-Riemannian manifold $(M, g_{\mu\nu})$ where
the metric $g_{\mu\nu}$ determines geodesic motion and the Kalb-Ramond 2-form $B_{\mu\nu}$ is an
auxiliary background field. Closed strings, however, possess two distinct topological quantum
numbers along any compact cycle: center-of-mass momentum $n \in \mathbb{Z}$ and string winding $w \in
\mathbb{Z}$.

In \textbf{Double Field Theory (DFT)} \cite{HullZwiebach2009, HohmHullZwiebach2010}, the tangent
bundle $TM$ and cotangent bundle $T^*M$ are unified into the generalized tangent bundle $\mathbb{T}M
= TM \oplus T^*M$. A generalized vector field $X^M \in \Gamma(\mathbb{T}M)$ combines a tangent vector
$v \in \Gamma(TM)$ and a 1-form $\xi \in \Gamma(T^*M)$, and $\mathbb{T}M$ carries the canonical
$O(D,D)$ split-signature metric
\begin{equation}
    \eta_{MN} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & \mathbf{1}_D \\ \mathbf{1}_D & 0 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad
    \langle X, Y \rangle_\eta = X^M \eta_{MN} Y^N = \xi_\mu u^\mu + \zeta_\mu v^\mu
\end{equation}
for $X = (v, \xi)$, $Y = (u, \zeta)$.

\begin{definition}[Generalized metric] \label{def:genmetric} \tierL
The gravitational and Kalb-Ramond fields combine into $\mathcal{H}_{MN} = \begin{pmatrix} g - B g^{-1}
B & B g^{-1} \\ -g^{-1} B & g^{-1} \end{pmatrix}$, parameterizing the coset
$\mathcal{H} \in O(D,D)/(O(D)\times O(D))$ with $\mathcal{H}^T\eta\mathcal{H}=\eta$,
$\mathcal{H}^2=\mathbf{1}_{2D}$.
\end{definition}

Generalized coordinate transformations are generated by the \textbf{Courant C-bracket} $[X,Y]_C =
[v,u]_{\mathrm{Lie}} + \big(\mathcal{L}_v\zeta - \mathcal{L}_u\xi - \tfrac12
d(\iota_v\zeta-\iota_u\xi)\big)$, whose Jacobiator is an exact 1-form, $\mathrm{Jac}(X,Y,Z) = [[X,Y]_C,
Z]_C + \text{cycl.} = d\,\mathrm{Nij}(X,Y,Z)$, with vanishing vector projection $\pi_{TM}(\mathrm{Jac})
= 0$; this guarantees closure of the generalized-diffeomorphism gauge algebra modulo trivial
transformations. Closure additionally requires the \textbf{strong section condition}
$\eta^{MN}\partial_M\Phi\,\partial_N\Psi = 0$, which in the physical frame ($\tilde\partial^i=0$)
collapses DFT onto ordinary 10D supergravity.

\begin{scopebox}
The Lean modules \texttt{DoubleFieldTheory.CourantAlgebroid} and
\texttt{DoubleFieldTheory.GeneralizedGeometry} certify (Tier A) the \emph{algebraic shape} of this
structure on a \textbf{1-dimensional scalar model}: a \texttt{CourantSection} is a pair of
\texttt{Int}s $(v,\alpha)$, and the bracket $[X,Y]_C$ reduces to $X.v\cdot Y.v - Y.v\cdot X.v$, which
is identically zero on scalars by commutativity of multiplication. The certified antisymmetry
(\texttt{cbracket\_antisymm}) and Jacobiator-vanishing (\texttt{jacobiator\_vector\_vanishes})
theorems are therefore true, but largely because every bracket in the scalar model is trivial; they
are \emph{consistency checks on the algebraic shape} of the definitions, not a formalization of the
Courant algebroid on $\mathbb{T}M = TM\oplus T^*M$ for an actual manifold $M$. Promoting this to a
genuine bundle-valued formalization (vector fields and 1-forms on $K3\times T^2$, with a nontrivial
Lie bracket) is future work and would require Mathlib's \texttt{VectorBundle} and differential-forms
libraries; see \Cref{sec:formalization}.
\end{scopebox}

\section{The Effective Dual Scale and Singularity Resolution}
\label{sec:effective-scale}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

The Hawking-Penrose singularity theorems show that under generic energy conditions a contracting
universe in general relativity inevitably reaches a curvature singularity: $a(t)\to 0 \implies
\mathcal{R}\to\infty$. String theory changes this conclusion kinematically. Closed strings
propagating on a circle of coordinate radius $R$ have mass spectrum $M^2 = n^2/R^2 + w^2R^2/\alpha'^2
+ \tfrac{2}{\alpha'}(N+\tilde N - 2)$, invariant under the Buscher T-duality
$\mathcal{T}: R \leftrightarrow \alpha'/R,\ n\leftrightarrow w$ \cite{Buscher1987}.

\begin{definition}[Effective dual scale] \label{def:reff} \tierL
The physical scale probed by a propagating string is governed by
\begin{equation}
    R_{\mathrm{eff}}(R) = R + \frac{\alpha'}{R} = \frac{R^2+\alpha'}{R}, \qquad \alpha'\text{-units: } \alpha'=1.
\end{equation}
This function --- also called the ``zero-point-length'' or minimal-length effective radius in the
T-duality/non-commutative-geometry literature --- and the string-gas cosmology it motivates were
introduced by Brandenberger and Vafa \cite{BrandenbergerVafa1989} and developed by Giveon, Porrati
and Rabinovici \cite{GiveonPorratiRabinovici1994}; we adopt their construction rather than proposing
a new one.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Global minimum of the effective scale] \label{thm:reff-min}

\noindent\tierL\ (real form)

\noindent\tierA\ (integer instance, below)
\smallskip

\noindent
For all $R\in\mathbb{R}^{+}$, $R_{\mathrm{eff}}(R) = R+\alpha'/R \ge 2\sqrt{\alpha'} > 0$, with
equality iff $R=\sqrt{\alpha'}$ (by AM--GM, or $f'(R)=1-\alpha'/R^2=0 \Rightarrow R=\sqrt{\alpha'}$,
$f''>0$).
\end{theorem}

\begin{scopebox}
Module \texttt{DualScaleValidation.UseCase1}, theorem \texttt{self\_dual\_is\_global\_minimum},
certifies (Tier A) the \textbf{integer instance} of \Cref{thm:reff-min}: for $R:\mathbb{N}$, $R\ge 1
\Rightarrow R^2+1\ge 2$ (i.e.\ $R_{\mathrm{eff}}(R)\cdot R \ge 2$ at $\alpha'=1$). It does not
formalize real radii, the square root, or the strict-minimality/uniqueness clause of
\Cref{thm:reff-min} --- those remain Tier L pending a Mathlib-based real-analytic proof
(\Cref{sec:formalization} gives a compiled-free sketch). Separately, theorem
\texttt{genesis\_no\_singularity} in module \texttt{DualScaleM24Formalization.DualScale.EffectiveMetric}
certifies that a \emph{piecewise} representation of $R_{\mathrm{eff}}$ on exact positive rationals
always has a positive numerator and denominator by construction of the underlying \texttt{PosScale}
type; this is true essentially by definition of the data structure and should not be read as an
independent proof of non-singularity.
\end{scopebox}

\begin{corollary}[Kinematic bounce] \label{cor:bounce} \tierL
As the coordinate radius contracts ($R\to0$), momentum modes decouple while winding modes become
light, and $R_{\mathrm{eff}}(R)\to\infty$: the string-frame probe distance does not go to zero. This
is a \emph{kinematic} statement about the mass spectrum, following Brandenberger--Vafa string-gas
cosmology \cite{BrandenbergerVafa1989}. It is not, by itself, a dynamical proof that a homogeneous
cosmology governed by some equations of motion for $a(t)$ must bounce smoothly through this regime
for \emph{every} choice of those equations of motion; that stronger claim would require specifying
and solving the dynamics, which is beyond the scope of the present formalization.
\end{corollary}

\section{Trans-Planckian Censorship: A Conditional Bound}
\label{sec:tcc}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

The \textbf{Trans-Planckian Censorship Conjecture (TCC)} \cite{BedroyaVafa2020} asserts that in any
consistent theory of quantum gravity, sub-Planckian quantum fluctuations can never cross the
cosmological horizon: $\tfrac{a_f}{a_i}\cdot\tfrac{\ell_{\mathrm{Pl}}}{d_H(t_f)} \le 1 \implies
\tfrac{a_f}{a_i} \le M_{\mathrm{Pl}}/H_{\mathrm{inf}}$.

\begin{theorem}[Wavelength floor] \label{thm:tcc} \tierC\ (as a TCC-satisfaction argument) \, /
\tierA\ (arithmetic instance, below)
Writing the physical wavelength of a comoving mode as $\lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}(R,\lambda_0) =
R_{\mathrm{eff}}(R)\cdot\lambda_0$, for integer $R\ge1$, $\lambda_0\ge1$ (Planck units), one has
$(R^2+1)\lambda_0 \ge 2 > \ell_{\mathrm{Pl}}$: this discrete family of modes never falls below the
Planck length.
\end{theorem}

\begin{scopebox}
\texttt{Lean5Corpus.Problems.DualScaleTCC.tcc\_cosmic\_protection\_contract} certifies (Tier A) the
conjunction of the arithmetic bound above with $p.\mathrm{planck\_mass}/p.\mathrm{hubble\_scale}\ge1$,
where \texttt{InflationParameters} is a structure carrying $\mathrm{hubble\_scale} <
\mathrm{planck\_mass}$ as a \emph{field} (an assumed hypothesis), not a derived consequence. The
theorem is therefore a conditional statement (``if the Hubble scale during inflation is sub-Planckian
and the comoving-mode/radius family lies in this discrete range, then this arithmetic inequality
holds''), not an unconditional proof that the TCC is satisfied ``regardless of the duration of
inflation'' for the dual-scale scenario's actual dynamics. We correct the earlier revision's
\emph{Corollary~(``Unconditional Horizon Protection'')} accordingly: horizon protection here is
conditional on the same discrete-radius, sub-Planckian-Hubble regime, not unconditional.
\end{scopebox}

\section{Compactification on \texorpdfstring{$K3\times T^2$}{K3 x T2}: Topology and the Moduli Obstruction}
\label{sec:compactification}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

We consider Type~II string theory on $M_{10}=\mathbb{R}^{3,1}\times K3\times T^2$. $K3$ is a
Calabi--Yau 2-fold with $SU(2)$ holonomy and $\chi(K3)=24$, $\sigma(K3)=-16$, $b_2(K3)=22$,
$h^{1,1}=20$, $h^{2,0}=1$, and middle homology $H_2(K3,\mathbb{Z})\cong 2(-E_8)\oplus 3U$. By the
Hirzebruch signature theorem specialized to a spin 4-manifold, the Dirac index is
$\mathrm{ind}(\slashed D) = -\sigma/8$ \cite{AtiyahSinger1968}; for $K3$,
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{ind}(\slashed D) = -\frac{-16}{8} = 2,
\end{equation}
consistently with the Todd-genus cross-check $\chi(\mathcal{O}_{K3})=\chi_{\mathrm{top}}/12=24/12=2$
required by Hirzebruch--Riemann--Roch for a Calabi--Yau surface (an earlier revision of this
manuscript instead wrote $\tfrac{1}{24}\int c_2 \times 2$, an unmotivated formula that happened to
give the right numerical answer; the corrected derivation above is the standard one). Type~IIA on
$K3$ yields 6D $\mathcal{N}=(1,1)$ supergravity (16 supercharges); further compactification on $T^2$
yields 4D $\mathcal{N}=4$ (still 16 supercharges) \cite{Witten1995}.

\begin{scopebox}
\texttt{DoubleFieldTheory.K3Topology} certifies (Tier A) the purely arithmetic instances of these
facts: $\chi(K3)=1-0+22-0+1=24$ from the Betti numbers, and $\mathrm{ind}(\slashed
D)=-(-16)/8=2$, both by \texttt{decide}. It does not formalize $K3$ as a manifold, its Betti numbers
as (co)homology dimensions, or the Atiyah--Singer index theorem itself --- those are Tier L,
standard, and correctly quoted, but not re-derived here.
\end{scopebox}

\subsection{The moduli-stabilization obstruction}
\label{sec:moduli-obstruction}
Type~II theory on $K3\times T^2$ preserves $\mathcal{N}=4$ supersymmetry in 4D. \textbf{This is the
central physical difficulty for any claim of full moduli stabilization on this background}: $\mathcal
N=4$ non-renormalization theorems forbid a perturbative or non-perturbative superpotential from
lifting the classical moduli space (the $K3$ sigma-model moduli, of real dimension $80+1$, and the
$T^2$ complex-structure/K\"ahler moduli) without additional ingredients --- background fluxes,
orientifolds/branes with associated tadpole cancellation, or explicit supersymmetry breaking. An
earlier revision of this paper asserted a ``Non-Perturbative Moduli Vacuum Stabilization Theorem''
claiming that the generalized Ricci scalar $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{DFT}}$ generates a potential
$V(\phi)=(\phi-\phi_0)^2$ stabilizing \emph{all} $K3\times T^2$ moduli with ``positive Hessian.'' We
withdraw that claim as stated: no derivation connects $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{DFT}}$ (\Cref{def:genmetric})
to this potential, and the potential itself does not engage the $80+1$-dimensional $K3$ moduli space
at all. What remains, honestly scoped, is the following.

\begin{proposition}[Toy potential --- corrected] \label{prop:toypotential} \tierA
Define, over $\mathbb{Z}$, $V(\phi,\phi_0) = (\phi-\phi_0)^2$. Then
\begin{align}
  \phi=\phi_0 &\implies V(\phi,\phi_0)=0 && \text{(\texttt{moduli\_vacuum\_stability})}\\
  V(\phi,\phi_0) &\ge 0 \text{ for all } \phi,\phi_0 && \text{(\texttt{moduli\_potential\_nonneg})}\\
  V(\phi,\phi_0) = 0 &\iff \phi=\phi_0 && \text{(\texttt{moduli\_potential\_zero\_iff}, uniqueness)}
\end{align}
\end{proposition}

\begin{scopebox}
An earlier revision of \texttt{UseCase1\_ModuliStabilization.lean} defined this potential over
\texttt{Nat} with ordinary (truncated) subtraction. Under \texttt{Nat} truncation, $\phi-\phi_0=0$
whenever $\phi<\phi_0$, so the earlier potential vanished on the entire ray $\phi\le\phi_0$ --- the
``unique minimum'' claimed in prose was not in fact unique in the formalized statement. The
definition has been changed to \texttt{Int}, and the genuinely new theorem
\texttt{moduli\_potential\_zero\_iff} above now proves the uniqueness that was previously only
asserted. This is a one-dimensional single-field toy model illustrating what a stabilizing potential
would look like near an isolated minimum; it is \emph{not} a formalization of the $K3\times T^2$
moduli-space potential, and does not by itself resolve the $\mathcal{N}=4$ non-renormalization
obstruction above. A genuine treatment would need to (a) introduce a concrete flux or brane
configuration, (b) verify its own consistency (flux quantization, tadpole cancellation), and (c)
derive a potential on the actual moduli space from it --- none of which is attempted in this
corpus. We list this as the top item in the roadmap of \Cref{sec:formalization}.
\end{scopebox}

\section{Mathieu \texorpdfstring{$M_{24}$}{M24} Moonshine, Holographic Codes, and the 27720 Integer}
\label{sec:moonshine}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

Eguchi, Ooguri, and Tachikawa (EOT) observed that the $K3$ elliptic genus decomposes into
$\mathcal{N}=4$ superconformal characters with multiplicities that are (twice) dimensions of
irreducible representations of the sporadic simple Mathieu group $M_{24}$
\cite{EguchiOoguriTachikawa2011}; this was subsequently proved in full by Gannon
\cite{Gannon2013}. Schematically (see \cite{EguchiOoguriTachikawa2011,Gannon2013} for the precise

\begin{equation}

is even (\texttt{chargeNorm\_\discretionary{}{}{}even}, since $\Gamma^{d,d}$ is even) and invariant
under all of $O(d,d;\mathbb Z)$ (\texttt{chargeNorm\_\discretionary{}{}{}invariant}). That these
generators \emph{generate} the full group $O(d,d;\mathbb Z)$ is Tier L (GPR l.~1422), not proved here.
\texttt{TDuality.Spectrum} shows every $g\in O(d,d;\mathbb Z)$ is invertible over $\mathbb Z$ with
inverse $\eta g^{\mathsf T}\eta$, again in $O(d,d;\mathbb Z)$
(\texttt{isODD\_\discretionary{}{}{}inv\_\discretionary{}{}{}isODD}), and an explicit \textbf{spectrum
equivalence}: for any background $H$ there is a bijection of the integer charge lattice under which
$(\text{mass}^2=Z^{\mathsf T}HZ,\ \text{level}=Z^{\mathsf T}\eta Z)$ for $H$ matches that of
$g^{\mathsf T}Hg$, charge by charge (\texttt{spectrum\_\discretionary{}{}{}equivalence}). Invariance of
the \emph{full} partition function (oscillators, modular integral; GPR l.~1417) is Tier L, not
addressed by this charge-level statement alone.

Two further modules deliberately narrow their claims. \texttt{TDuality.Mirror} proves, on $T^2$ with
$T=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ and $\Theta=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$,
that $D_0\cdot\mathrm{diag}(T,(T^{\mathsf T})^{-1})\cdot D_0=(g_\Theta)^{\mathsf T}$
(\texttt{mirror\_\discretionary{}{}{}conjugates\_\discretionary{}{}{}tauShift}): the factorized
duality conjugates the $\tau\mapsto\tau+1$ generator into a pure $B$-field shift. That this is
``factorized duality = mirror symmetry for a complex torus'' is Tier L (GPR l.~344); \emph{the sign of
the induced $\rho$-shift is convention-dependent and is not claimed}. \texttt{TDuality.SL2Product}
proves $AJA^{\mathsf T}=\det(A)\cdot J$ for $2\times2$ integer $A$ and that a basis change with
$\det A=1$ commutes with every $\rho$-translation $g_{tJ}$
(\texttt{basisChange\_\discretionary{}{}{}comm\_\discretionary{}{}{}thetaShift}) --- this one direction
only is Tier A. That the commutator is \emph{nonzero} when $\det A\ne1$ was checked symbolically with
sympy, not in Lean, and \textbf{is not presented as Tier A}. GPR's full statement for ``the $d=2$
example,'' that the duality group is $SL(2,\mathbb Z)\times SL(2,\mathbb Z)\otimes_S[\mathbb
Z_2\times\mathbb Z_2]$ (ll.~1874--1884), is likewise \textbf{not claimed in full}; only the
$SL(2,\mathbb Z)_\tau$ commuting with $\rho$-translations, above, is formalized.

\subsection{Double Field Theory over \texorpdfstring{$\mathbb R$}{R}, every \texorpdfstring{$d$}{d}}
\label{sec:stream2-dft}
\texttt{DFT.GeneralizedMetric} formalizes the Hull--Zwiebach generalized metric
\cite{HullZwiebach2009} (eq.~(2.17), ll.~662--672),
\begin{equation}
\mathcal H(G,B)=\begin{pmatrix}G-BG^{-1}B & BG^{-1}\\-G^{-1}B & G^{-1}\end{pmatrix},
\end{equation}
and proves $(\eta\mathcal H)^2=1$ for \emph{any}
antisymmetric $B$, given only that $G$ is invertible
(\texttt{etaR\_\discretionary{}{}{}genMetric\_\discretionary{}{}{}sq}) --- sharper than HZ's own
stated constraint $\mathcal H^{-1}=\eta\mathcal H\eta$, which assumed more. It proves $\mathcal H$
symmetric for symmetric $G$, antisymmetric $B$ (\texttt{genMetric\_\discretionary{}{}{}symm}); that
\textbf{T-duality is metric inversion}, $\eta\,\mathcal H(G,0)\,\eta=\mathcal H(G^{-1},0)$
(\texttt{tduality\_\discretionary{}{}{}inverts\_\discretionary{}{}{}metric}); that the mass form
$Z^{\mathsf T}\mathcal HZ$ is covariant under any $g$
(\texttt{massForm\_\discretionary{}{}{}covariant}); and that on a circle ($G=R^2$, $B=0$) this mass
form equals half of Stream~1's $P_L^2+P_R^2$ (\texttt{massForm\_\discretionary{}{}{}circle}) --- so
Stream~2's DFT layer and Stream~1's circle computation provably agree. \texttt{DFT.BShift} proves
$g_\Theta\cdot\mathcal H(G,B)\cdot g_\Theta^{\mathsf T}=\mathcal H(G,B+\Theta)$ for antisymmetric
$\Theta$ (\texttt{genMetric\_\discretionary{}{}{}bshift}): integer $B$-field shifts act as $O(d,d)$
conjugation. The sign/transpose convention in this identity was checked with sympy \emph{before}
formalization --- exactly one of four candidate variants holds --- and the Lean theorem records the
one that does. \texttt{DFT.SectionCondition} shows the level-matching condition
$L_0-\bar L_0=N-\bar N-p\cdot w=0$ (HZ eq.~(1.3), l.~231) is, at $N=\bar N$, exactly $n\cdot w=0$
(\texttt{levelMatching\_\discretionary{}{}{}iff}); that the pure-momentum and pure-winding frames are
both totally $\eta$-null, i.e.\ solve the section condition
(\texttt{momentumFrame\_\discretionary{}{}{}isSection}, \texttt{windingFrame\_\discretionary{}{}{}isSection};
HZ ll.~339--342, 3930--3940); that $\eta$ maps one frame to the other
(\texttt{eta\_\discretionary{}{}{}momentum\_\discretionary{}{}{}to\_\discretionary{}{}{}winding}); and
that $O(d,d;\mathbb Z)$ maps sections to sections
(\texttt{isSection\_\discretionary{}{}{}image}).

\subsection{The dual-scale bound, generalized}
\label{sec:stream2-dualscale}
\texttt{DualScale.TraceBound} lifts \Cref{thm:reff-min} from the integer circle case to any
positive-definite $G$ and any dimension $d$. Define $\mathcal D(G):=\operatorname{tr}\mathcal
H(G,0)=\operatorname{tr}G+\operatorname{tr}G^{-1}$. Then $\mathcal D(G^{-1})=\mathcal D(G)$
(\texttt{dualScale\_\discretionary{}{}{}inv}, T-duality invariance), and
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal D(G)\ \ge\ 2d \quad\text{for every positive-definite } G,
\end{equation}
proved (\texttt{dualScale\_\discretionary{}{}{}ge}) from $\operatorname{tr}\big((G-1)G^{-1}(G-1)\big)\ge0$
expanding to $\operatorname{tr}G+\operatorname{tr}G^{-1}-2d\ge0$ --- no eigenvalue decomposition is
used --- with equality at the self-dual point $G=1$
(\texttt{dualScale\_\discretionary{}{}{}one}). On a circle, $\mathcal D(R^2)=(R+1/R)^2-2$
(\texttt{dualScale\_\discretionary{}{}{}circle}), recovering $R+1/R\ge2$
(\texttt{circle\_\discretionary{}{}{}effective\_\discretionary{}{}{}scale\_\discretionary{}{}{}ge\_\discretionary{}{}{}two}) ---
the same real-analytic statement behind Stream~1's
\texttt{EffectiveMetric.\discretionary{}{}{}genesis\_\discretionary{}{}{}no\_\discretionary{}{}{}singularity}
($R_{\mathrm{eff}}=R+\alpha'/R\ge2\sqrt{\alpha'}$), but now proved directly over $\mathbb R$ rather
than only instantiated on integers. As with \Cref{cor:bounce}, any cosmological reading (singularity
resolution, a minimum length) remains \textbf{Tier C}; \texttt{TraceBound} is a statement about a
matrix trace, not about a dynamical spacetime.

\subsection{Flux and tadpole on \texorpdfstring{$K3\times T^2$}{K3xT2} / \texorpdfstring{$K3\times K3$}{K3xK3}}

\subsection{What is in the repository}
The original, Mathlib-free \textbf{core} comprises \textbf{51 Lean~4 files} and \textbf{554
declarations} (\texttt{theorem} / \texttt{lemma} / \texttt{def} / \texttt{structure} /
\texttt{inductive} / \texttt{instance} / \texttt{abbrev}) on toolchain
\texttt{leanprover/\discretionary{}{}{}lean4:\discretionary{}{}{}v4.33.1}, across five libraries:
\begin{center}
\footnotesize\ttfamily
DoubleFieldTheory \quad DualScaleValidation \quad DualScaleM24Formalization \quad
StringTheoryFoundation \quad Lean5Corpus
\end{center}
(note: the library named \texttt{StringTheoryFoundation} above, singular, is one of these five
Mathlib-free libraries and is distinct from \texttt{StringTheoryFormalization}, plural-suffixed,
described next). \texttt{lake build} completes all 61 jobs for these five libraries with no errors
and no \texttt{sorry} warnings, using only Lean~4 core (\texttt{Init}), with \emph{no Mathlib}. This
is the direct explanation for why every proof cited above \Cref{sec:stream2} is over \texttt{Nat},
\texttt{Int}, or an ad hoc rational-pair structure rather than over $\mathbb{R}$ --- core Lean does
not provide real numbers, manifolds, or differential-geometric structures. We regard stating this
limitation plainly as more useful than the earlier revision's blanket claim of ``100\% certified
physics.''

\textbf{As of this revision, the repository as a whole is larger than this core.} Two further
libraries are Mathlib-dependent (the project's \texttt{lake-manifest.json} now lists Mathlib and its
transitive dependencies, pinned at tag \texttt{v4.33.1}): \texttt{StringTheoryFormalization} (35
files, Stream~1's sixth library, not a \texttt{lake} default target until it built clean; 89 of the
theorems in the 326-theorem Stream~1 audit total of \Cref{sec:stream2}) and
\texttt{DualScaleStream2} (19 modules, 99 theorems, \Cref{sec:stream2}). \textbf{Depending on
Mathlib does not, by itself, weaken any Tier A claim}: the axiom audits of both libraries
(\texttt{tools/axiom\_\discretionary{}{}{}audit.py}, \Cref{sec:stream2}) show 0 failing declarations
and no axiom beyond Lean's three standard ones, the same bar as \Cref{sec:tiers} sets for the
Mathlib-free core. What Mathlib buys is exactly the real numbers, matrices, and linear algebra that
the five-library core above cannot express --- which is why \Cref{sec:stream2}'s dual-scale bound
(\Cref{sec:stream2-dualscale}) is stated over $\mathbb R$ rather than only instantiated on integers.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

This paper is narrower than its earlier revision claimed. It kernel-certifies (Tier A) a set of
exact integer and rational identities --- the positivity of a dual-scale bound on integer radii, $K3$

real bug) --- and it states, without conflating tiers, which further claims are established

which are this paper's own conjectures (Tier C: the zero-free-parameter proposal and its three
numerical phenomenology relations, one of which, the dark-energy prediction, is already in tension
with DESI DR2). We consider this an improvement, not a retreat: a claim that survives being checked
against its own formal artifact and against current data is worth more than a stronger claim that
does not.
