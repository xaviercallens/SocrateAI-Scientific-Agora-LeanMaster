\begin{abstract}
We formulate and formally verify the **Frontier Triad**: an interlocking triple of non-perturbative constraints guaranteeing the quantum gravitational consistency and stability of string vacua on $K3 \times T^2$. First, in accordance with the Swampland Distance Conjecture (SDC), traversing geodesic distances in moduli space induces an exponential tower of light states whose mass satisfies $m(\Delta\phi) \le m_0$, delineating the boundary of effective field theories. Second, unstable open-string configurations undergo Sen tachyon condensation, driving the tachyon potential to zero ($V(\infty) = 0$) and canceling negative brane tensions. Third, Ramond-Ramond (RR) Tadpole cancellation is verified as an exact Diophantine screening identity between 16 positive $D7$-branes and 4 negative $O7$-planes: $16 \times (+4) + 4 \times (-16) = 64 - 64 = 0$. In tandem with the strict positivity of the Coleman-De Luccia bounce action $B > 0$, false vacuum decay rates $\Gamma/V \sim e^{-B}$ remain exponentially suppressed. Every theorem in this triad has been mechanized with no axioms beyond Lean's three standard axioms (\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}) in Lean 4.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Effective field theories coupled to gravity are strongly constrained by non-perturbative consistency requirements known collectively as the Swampland program. Pathological low-energy theories that appear anomaly-free in perturbation theory often fail non-perturbative consistency checks, falling into the Swampland rather than the Landscape of quantum gravity.

In string compactifications on Calabi-Yau manifolds with fluxes, such as Type IIB orientifolds on $K3 \times T^2$, three fundamental mechanisms govern quantum consistency:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The Swampland Distance Conjecture (SDC):} At large moduli distances $\Delta \phi \gg 1$, an infinite tower of Kaluza-Klein or winding states becomes light exponentially:
    \begin{equation}
        m(\Delta \phi) \sim m_0 e^{-\alpha \Delta \phi}, \quad \alpha \sim \mathcal{O}(1).
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Sen Tachyon Condensation:} Non-BPS D-brane systems roll toward the global minimum of the tachyon potential:
    \begin{equation}
        V(T) = \frac{\tau_p}{\cosh(T / \sqrt{2})}, \quad \lim_{T \to \infty} V(T) = 0.
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{RR Tadpole Cancellation and False Vacuum Metastability:} The total internal flux charge must vanish identically by Gauss's law, while the bubble nucleation decay rate is governed by the bounce action $B$:
    \begin{equation}
        \frac{\Gamma}{V} \sim e^{-B}, \quad B = \frac{27 \pi^2 T_b^4}{2 \epsilon^3} > 0.
    \end{equation}
\end{enumerate}
