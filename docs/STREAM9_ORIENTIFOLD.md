# Stream 9 — orientifolds of `T⁶`: the lattice layer (2026-09-20)

**Status (`v3.31.0`):** one Tier A file, `DualScaleStream2/Orientifold/NarainT6.lean` (12 theorems). Everything
else below is a plan. Nothing here claims a vacuum, a spectrum, or `N = 1`.

## 0. Why this stream exists

`K3 × T²` gives `N = 4` and no chirality (Stream 7), and Stream 8 closed the question of *which* `K3 × T²` without
producing a vacuum: trapping selects `SO(44)`, a point that is not a product (§12), and the maximal-symmetry
model prefers the `i` complex structure over `ω` (§14). Orientifolds of `T⁶` are the standard route to `N = 1`
whose ingredients stay finite and integral — lattices, involutions, integer charges — and therefore certifiable at
the same standard as the rest of this repository.

## 1. What is proved (Tier A)

| Theorem | Statement |
|---|---|
| `gamma66_symm`, `gamma66_evenDiag` | `Γ₆,₆ = U⁶` is symmetric with even diagonal |
| `gamma66_mul_self`, `gamma66_unimodular` | `Γ₆,₆ · Γ₆,₆ = 1`, hence unimodular by the `Lattice.Basic` certificate route (an exhibited integer inverse, never an asserted determinant) |
| `gamma66_signature` | six explicit vectors of norm `+2` (`eᵢ + fᵢ`) and six of norm `−2` (`eᵢ − fᵢ`): signature `(6, 6)` |
| `theta_isometries` | the three non-trivial elements `θ₁, θ₂, θ₃` of `ℤ₂ × ℤ₂` (each flipping four real coordinates) are isometries, are involutions, and compose as `θ₁θ₂ = θ₃` |
| `parity_antiisometry` | worldsheet parity `Ω` satisfies `Ωᵀ G Ω = −G`: an **anti**-isometry |
| `orientifold_involutions`, `orientifold_antiisometries` | each `Ω θᵢ` squares to the identity and is an anti-isometry |
| `fixed_point_counts` | `2⁴ = 16`, `2⁶ = 64`: the fixed loci of one `θᵢ` and of the full reflection |
| `plane_charge_total` | `2^{9−p} · (−2^{p−5}) = −16` for `p = 3, 5, 7, 9`; for `p = 3`, 64 planes of charge `−1/4` |
| `consistent_with_O7_frame` | the same `−16` in the `T²/ℤ₂` frame already formalized: `4 · (−4) + 16 · 1 = 0` |

**The one structural point worth stating in words.** `Ω` is an *anti*-isometry of the Narain form, not an
isometry. Exchanging left- and right-movers reverses the sign of `p_L² − p_R²`. So an orientifold group is not a
subgroup of `O(6,6;ℤ)`: it is generated by isometries `θᵢ` together with one element that negates the form. Any
later formalization of the orientifold projection has to respect that, and the theorem
`parity_antiisometry` is what keeps it honest.

T-duality `O(d,d;ℤ)`); Polchinski TASI `polchinski_tasi_dbranes_hep-th_9611050.txt` ll. 1968–1984 eq. (93)
(`O_p` charge `−2^{p−5}`, `2^{9−p}` planes); Sen `hep-th_9605150.txt` ll. 238–250 with the v3.21.0 correction in

## 2. What is deliberately not claimed

* That any particular `Γ` yields `N = 1`, or anything about the spectrum or its chirality.
* That the charge arithmetic is a *solution*: it is the budget. A solution needs brane positions and, with flux,
  the `½N_flux + N_D3` relation already formalized for the `K3 × T²/ℤ₂` frame.
* Any connection to the dual-scale proposal. Stream 9 is a change of compactification, not a continuation of the
  `K3 × T²` hypothesis; Stream 8's conclusions do not transfer to it.
